# DerpMeter app artwork

- `DerpMeter.png`: the DerpMeter terminal-face logo, shared with the website.
- `Providers/claude.png`, `openai.png`, `gemini.png`: 64 px exports of the Claude, OpenAI and Gemini marks from [LobeHub Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons) (MIT, license in `Providers/LICENSE.txt`). OpenAI is inverted for the dark surface.
- `Providers/antigravity.png`: a 64 px export of Google's official Antigravity product mark (https://antigravity.google/assets/image/antigravity-logo.png, 2026-10-22). It is a Google trademark and not covered by the LobeHub MIT license.
- `Fonts/`: static [Geist](https://github.com/vercel/geist-font) Regular and Bold, under the SIL Open Font License (`Fonts/OFL.txt`). They are registered only for the lifetime of the app process.
